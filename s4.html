Section 4

Current Platform Baseline

Knowledge base review of existing Nomni capabilities.

Based on a structured review of the Nomni support knowledge base (~94 articles across Nomni Procure, Nomni Supply, and Developers collections). Establishes the baseline, corrects assumptions about what is net-new, and identifies improvement areas.

4.1 Buyer side — Nomni Procure

AreaCurrent capabilities
Ordering & receivingOrder creation (mobile + web); fill-to-par with POS integration; order approvals incl. bulk approval; shortage recording; GRN; downloadable receiving lists; supplier notification of incomplete/wrong deliveries; promo codes.
InvoicesUpload by photo, PDF, email-in; AI invoice digitisation; approve/reject workflow with rectification; expense category assignment; PEPPOL e-invoice receiving and PEPPOL ID registration (Buyer Hub).
Items, suppliers & recipesMarket list item add/remove; item prices, custom names, product codes, tags; price-change notifications; Group SKU (multi-outlet item normalisation); supplier add/edit; Recipes with inventory tracking.
InventoryInventory lists and items (desktop + mobile); UOM with conversion rates; stock counts with drafts and multi-unit recording; inventory adjustments; invoice-driven stock quantity updates; stock transfers.
Reports & dataSpending reports; 2-Way and 3-Way Match; Price Changes; Order Receiving; COGS and Theoretical COGS; Inventory On Hand; supplier data export; AI insights (BETA).
IntegrationsNomni POS incl. POS variant mapping; Xero, QuickBooks Online, MYOB Business.
Accounts & securityUser management; biometric (fingerprint/face) unlock; company verification.

4.2 Supplier side — Nomni Supply

AreaCurrent capabilities
Order managementOrdering rules; order acknowledgement; pick lists, picking slips, packing lists; cancel/reject orders; create orders on behalf of customers.
Catalogue & inventoryItem/catalogue management; supplier-side inventory; tax rate configuration.

Reports & mobileOrders Received and Sales reports; supplier mobile app with sales-goal dashboard.
